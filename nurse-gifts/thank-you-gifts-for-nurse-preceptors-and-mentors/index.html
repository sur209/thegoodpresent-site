Thank You Gifts for Nurse Preceptors and Mentors

Thoughtful, workplace-appropriate ways to thank the preceptor or mentor who supported a nursing student or new nurse.

Updated November 3, 2026 · Edited by Alejandro

A Handwritten Thank-You CardA Shared Memory Photo BookAn Engraved Desk Nameplate

You’ve finished orientation or a clinical placement and want to thank the preceptor who helped you find your footing. Let the message carry the meaning, then choose a modest, professional gesture that suits the relationship, occasion, and recipient’s routine. Shared appreciation can work well for a cohort, while a small desk or home keepsake may feel right for an individual; keep the sentiment personal, the choice non-clinical, and any workplace gift policies in mind.
