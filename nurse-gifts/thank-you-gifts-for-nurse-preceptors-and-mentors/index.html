Thoughtful Gifts for Nurse Preceptors

Eight thoughtful ways to thank a nurse preceptor, with modest individual gestures and shared appreciation from classmates that keep personal gratitude at the center.

Updated November 9, 2026 · Edited by Alejandro

A Handwritten Thank-You CardA Shared Photo and Message BookAn Engraved Desk Nameplate

These gift ideas for nurse preceptors help you thank the mentor who made time for your questions during a clinical placement or orientation. A thoughtful gesture can acknowledge that support while keeping the relationship professional. Consider their interests, the space they have at home or at a desk, and whether they prefer keepsakes or something to enjoy. If classmates share your gratitude, contributing personal messages together can make a collective thank-you meaningful without expecting everyone to spend. Whether you give individually or as a group, let a specific memory of their teaching carry the sentiment. A sincere written thank-you can also stand on its own. Recommendation 1

A Handwritten Thank-You Card

Tell your preceptor which teaching moment stayed with you and how their guidance helped you grow. A personal message can stand alone as a complete thank-you at the end of orientation or a clinical placement, with no additional gift needed. If a nurse practitioner preceptor guided an advanced practice placement, mention a moment when their teaching made a difference.

Why it fits

An individual note gives a mentor something specific to remember about your time together while keeping the gesture modest and professional.

Best for
A preceptor whose patience or teaching made a lasting impression
How to choose
Choose a simple card with enough blank space for your message. Mention a teaching habit, encouragement, or lesson you valued, and close with a sincere thank-you. Leave out patient details.
